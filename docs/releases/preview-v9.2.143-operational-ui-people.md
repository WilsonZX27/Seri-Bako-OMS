# v9.2.143 Preview candidate — Operational UI and People workflows

## Scope

Responsive transfer actions, simpler forms and notices, distinct leave colours, central RO management, and permission-scoped managed stock destinations. Retains the 9.2.142 offboarding access guard. Chef import-catalog accepts the Chef role while existing branch, permission and employment-date checks remain.

## Activation boundaries

Paid emergency leave requires its separately approved backend capability and remains unavailable without it. Asset batch and numbering migrations remain inactive. This release does not apply SQL, create locations, submit stock transfers, alter employee accounts, or rewrite historical holidays or payroll.

## Verification

Final integrated checks are recorded separately before publication. Production receives only its independently approved minimal patches.
